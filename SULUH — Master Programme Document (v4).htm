0.1Authorship & Origin

Why this section exists

SULUH is not a vendor proposal. It is a conceived programme whose architecture, design principles, and research outputs originate from a single author. This section makes that origin explicit — and explains why the programme's institutional adoption at UPSI is structured as a partnership, not a procurement.

Origin of the SULUH Programme

The SULUH Ecosystem — comprising the IsRAG (Isnad-chain Retrieval Augmented Generation), Dewan Council (multi-LLM deliberation), and SULUH AI (sovereign orchestration platform) packages — was conceived and designed by Putra Nasution, an independent AI architect and researcher. The three open-source packages, the seven immutable design principles (§10), the three-track cloud→sovereign→edge model (§7), the federated swarm algorithms (MA3, APA-EF, LES-GA), and the five-paper publication plan (§18) are his authored works, developed prior to and independent of any UPSI engagement.

The connection to UPSI is through Dr. Suzani binti Mohamad Samuri, Faculty of Meta, whose research collaboration established the institutional bridge. The programme's adoption at UPSI is enabled through a research-collaboration agreement that brings Putra Nasution as Lead Architect and Founding Director of the proposed UPSI AI Lab (§19).

Outsider status — disclosed and framed as a feature

Putra Nasution is not a UPSI employee and is not a Malaysian citizen. Rather than treating this as a constraint, the programme is structured so that outsider status is a structural advantage:

  • Provenance from outside the institution — the architecture, code, and publications predate the UPSI engagement. UPSI acquires a fully authored IP position without internal R&D lead time.
  • No internal politics — as an external architect, PN is not aligned with any faculty, division, or administrative faction. Governance runs through the Steering Committee (§15), not through the architect's organisational position.
  • IP ownership clarity — the three open-source packages are Apache 2.0; UPSI's institutional adaptations (harness code, golden sets, deployment artifacts) are UPSI-owned. The split is clean and contractual.
  • Founding Director continuity — embedding PN as Director of the UPSI AI Lab (§19) is the contractual vehicle that converts an external collaboration into an institutional capability. Removing PN from the programme severs the algorithmic authorship and paper plan (§17 Risk Register: "PI / architect bandwidth").
How to read this document. Every mention of "Lead Architect" or "SULUH Architecture Team" in this document refers to Putra Nasution and the engineering team he leads. The programme does not assume Malaysian-ness in tone or legal posture. The governing law of the research-collaboration agreement is Malaysian; the governing IP licence is Apache 2.0; the governing compliance regime is PDPA 2010. 2Executive Briefing

The Bottom Line

UPSI's academic operations have outgrown their intelligence model. Lecturers spend whole weekends grading without producing accreditation evidence; curriculum compliance takes weeks of manual cross-checking; at-risk students are discovered a semester too late; staff analytics are static. SULUH embeds a conversational, agentic AI layer across these workflows — every answer cited, every consequential action human-approved, every byte sovereign.

SULUH does not replace UPSI's operational systems. It augments them. UniSIS, UniHRIS, MYRIMS, and the LMS remain the systems of record; the AI layer reads through a governed data fabric and writes back only through named human approval gates.

The programme's central bet is architectural: one intelligence supply chain serving four harnesses beats four isolated AI pilots. The Consolidated Matrix analysis found the seven original faculty proposals were secretly one dependency graph — duplicate scrapers on the same HR databases, twin grading stacks, two unaware at-risk systems. Merging them eliminates an estimated 40% of backend engineering and, more importantly, makes governance enforceable in one place instead of seven.

Waiting 12 months means another Malaysian university publishes this first.

  • Hours lost forever — the ~61,000-hour annual opportunity (§3) does not accrue while the system is undeployed; a one-year delay is a one-year write-off of it.

Why 35B MoE, not 70B or 100B dense

A 70B dense model at FP16 needs ~140 GB of weights plus 100+ GB of KV cache under concurrent load — overflowing a 256 GB node once OS overhead and co-resident models are counted. The 35B MoE activates only ~3B parameters per token, achieving comparable task quality in a ~20 GB serving footprint — leaving the cluster 6× headroom for QLoRA jobs, evaluation runs, and burst traffic. Full-scale 100B pre-training remains the Phase-2 vision, contingent on national compute partnership; it is upside, not dependency.

Three tracks, matched to what each tier can verifiably do

Track A — Cloud teacher (API, zero PII). Kimi K3 and Claude Opus 4.8 generate curriculum-grounded synthetic training data from anonymised rubrics and model answers, and arbitrate quality on the golden set. Anonymised curriculum artifacts out, synthetic corpus back in; ~RM 3–5K over 12 months; no raw student or staff data ever leaves UPSI — enforced in code (§13).

Track B — Sovereign teacher (on-premise 35B). Staged adaptation: synthetic corpus (M1–2) → continual pre-training on Malay/English academic corpora (M2–4) → instruction tuning on grading and advising tasks (M4–6) → federated adaptation on faculty-local documents (M6–9) → DPO alignment (M9–10) → accreditation-readiness evaluation (M10–12). QLoRA makes every stage feasible on the cluster's unified memory.

Track C — Federated student (10,000 laptops). Qwen3.5-0.8B/2B distilled from the teacher, then adapted via federated LoRA under APA-EF differential privacy. Laptops train models they can genuinely hold; only adapter deltas move.

Foundational proof: Psyche

Psyche (Nous Research) demonstrated distributed transformer training across untrusted internet participants using coordinator/client consensus over ordinary connections — proving the core premise SULUH's federated track relies on: no high-speed interconnect or coherent memory space required.

SULUH operates under a strictly easier trust assumption — all 10,000 devices are institutionally owned and SSO-enrolled — which unlocks what untrusted networks cannot have: APA-EF's calibrated privacy parameters (no untrusted node can hold sparsification secrets) and LES-GA's shared curriculum knowledge base (which only institutional ownership makes possible). SULUH's contribution is therefore not re-proving distributed training. It is the safe, private, educational deployment pattern inside a sovereign institutional boundary — which is exactly what the Federated Swarm paper (§18) claims, no more, and defensibly.

11.2 · Curriculum Knowledge API — PPA · Dr. Amelia · Low/Internal

Mission. CURATOR-AI: automate KPT/MQA compliance auditing for programme development, and expose the resulting CLO–Bloom knowledge as the versioned API (contract C2) every grading engine depends on. The smallest harness with the highest leverage — it ships first, because H3 cannot compute CLO attainment without it.

Design spine. Two-speed pipeline: a deterministic rule engine lints the checkable live in the editor (credit structure, SLT hours); an async NLP auditor handles the semantic (CLO-to-standard alignment, Bloom classification) and returns findings cited to the exact clause. Nothing publishes to C2 without Dean/TDAA approval — the API serves only human-ratified mappings. Grounded in constructive alignment (Biggs) and the revised Bloom taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl).

Versioning is a safety feature. Downstream grading pins a mapping version. If PPA revises a CLO mid-semester, H3 continues grading against the pinned version until the Course Coordinator explicitly migrates — no silent grade-basis changes, the failure mode accreditation auditors care about most.

Acceptance bar. ≥ 90% Bloom/CLO agreement with an expert-labelled 300-CLO set; 100% citation resolvability; consumer-driven contract tests from H3/H4 in CI; POC = one MQA Level-6 programme (Standard: Education) authored, audited, approved, and served over C2 end-to-end.

11.3 · Generative Instructional Engine — Pusat ICT/BHEA/BHEP · Dr. Mohd Muslim, Dr. Ahmad Wiraputra · Medium/PDPA

Mission. One shared generative pipeline with two faces: rubric-based AI grading with cited evidence spans (MyGuru, internal LMS) and a grounded AI tutor with dropout prediction (MOOC, external). The matrix found the two proposals had "identical technical DNA"; merging them shares one model stack, one ops team, one evaluation harness.

Evidence base. Intelligent tutoring achieves effect sizes near human tutoring (d ≈ 0.76 vs 0.79 — VanLehn), against Bloom's two-sigma benchmark; LLM judges exceed 80% agreement with human raters on rubric-anchored tasks (Zheng et al.); MOOC completion sits near 3% at scale (Reich & Ruipérez-Valiente, Science) — the uplift target is +50% relative in AI-tutored cohorts.

Design spine. Strict JSON grading contract — {criterion_id, score, evidence_spans[], rationale, confidence} — where evidence spans must be verbatim substrings of the submission (non-matching spans zero the confidence). Confidence < 0.75 or injection flags divert to mandatory re-review. The tutor answers only from the approved course corpus or abstains. There is no code path from a draft grade to UniSIS that bypasses the lecturer — a continuous canary test attempts it and must fail 100% of runs.

Acceptance bar. ≥ 85% panel agreement; ≤ 5% hallucination on the golden set; < 3 s grading / < 1 s chat; Malay/English scoring parity; pre-launch double-marking study (engine vs two lecturers, 300 essays) — publishable as part of the deployment paper.

11.4 · Unified Student Intelligence Platform — BHEA/BHEP · Dr. Vasanthan, Pn. Nurul Ashikin, Jason Wong · High/Confidential

Mission. End fragmented student records and late discovery. One canonical student model (contract C4) under three role surfaces: myACADmate (student PWA — progress, cited handbook chat, semester pathway), UPSI Buddy (advisor/BHEP bento dashboard — alerts, coordination, talent mapping with EDGE and Pulami data), and a counselor-only wellbeing view.

Design spine. Hybrid risk: a calibrated ML dropout predictor plus deterministic rule triggers, every flag carrying explainable top features. The intervention workflow is a first-class surface — flag → PTJ/PIC validation (24 h) → tracked ticket — because the early-warning literature (Course Signals; Jayaprakash et al.) shows timeliness and intervention design, not model accuracy, are the binding constraints. Wellbeing data lives in a separately-keyed vault only the counselor surface can decrypt, on a 4-hour SLA.

No automated consequences. The system never withholds a registration, notifies a parent, or alters a record from a model output. Predictions inform humans; humans act. This is the harness's core ethical commitment (Slade & Prinsloo) and a hard line in code.

Acceptance bar. ≥ 70% PTJ-validated flag precision; ≥ 20% faster signal-to-intervention; zero role-leakage findings in penetration testing (counselor data must never render in an advisor DOM); k-anonymity floor on every aggregate tile.
